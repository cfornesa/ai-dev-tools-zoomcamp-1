## Investigation findings
- Reference (augment-humankind `algorithms/MovementSonification.md` §12; react-node `sonic-controller.js`) carries authored per-piece `sonic_params` (tempo 40-220, 9 scales, 7 instruments, feel, extras: default_volume, per-voice instruments, filter type/cutoff/resonance, octave range, effects chain, ambient sample), generated by AI from a mood and validated leniently. Visitors get volume, octave, instrument and effects. The reference has no visitor BPM slider, oscillator or ADSR control; those are owner additions.
- This repo: schema has only `sound: boolean`; `sonicEngine.ts` has a shared volume, per-voice instrument (#345), a fixed 2000 Hz lowpass, a fixed `2n` ambient loop over a hard-coded scale, and a computer-keyboard piano map. No authored sounds, no AI sound generation, no on-screen keyboard, no tempo/scale/filter/oscillator/envelope/effects controls.
- Conclusion: the workflow does not adequately account for specific sounds; #832 covers making existing sound work in live pieces and ZIPs only.
